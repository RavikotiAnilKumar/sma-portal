Glossary of Terms

Atrophy
Waste away, typically due to the degeneration of cells, or become vestigial during evolution.
Autosomal Recessive
A way a genetic trait or condition can be passed down from parent to child.
Motor Neurons
Nerve cells that send messages from the brain to the muscles.
SMN Protein
Survival Motor Neuron protein, essential for the health of motor neurons.
