Treatment and Support

Disease-Modifying Therapies

In recent years, several groundbreaking treatments have been approved by regulatory agencies like the FDA and EMA. These treatments aim to increase the production of the SMN protein.

  • Gene Replacement Therapy: Delivers a new, functional copy of the SMN1 gene.
  • SMN2 Splicing Modifiers: Helps the SMN2 "backup" gene produce more functional SMN protein.

Multidisciplinary Care

Respiratory Support

Maintaining lung health through breathing exercises, cough assistance machines, or ventilation support.

Nutritional Support

Ensuring adequate caloric intake and proper swallowing management to prevent aspiration.

Physical & Occupational Therapy

Exercises to maintain range of motion, muscle strength, and independence in daily activities.

Orthopedic Care

Monitoring for scoliosis and joint issues, often requiring braces or surgical intervention.

Note on Medication

Treatment choices are highly individualized. Please consult with a neurologist or SMA specialist to discuss the best options for your specific situation.
