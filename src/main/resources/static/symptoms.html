Signs and Symptoms

Symptoms of SMA can vary greatly depending on the type and age of onset. Early recognition is key to better outcomes.

💪

Muscle Weakness

Progressive weakness in the proximal muscles (shoulders, hips, thighs, and upper back). This is often the first sign noticed by parents.

🚶

Movement Difficulties

Delays in reaching motor milestones. Infants may have difficulty lifting their heads or sitting up, while older children may have trouble climbing stairs.

🫁

Breathing Issues

Weakness in the intercostal muscles (between the ribs) can lead to a bell-shaped torso and shallow breathing, increasing the risk of pneumonia.

🍽️

Swallowing Problems

Bulbar muscle weakness can cause difficulty in sucking, swallowing, and chewing, often requiring nutritional support or feeding tubes.

Other Signs Include:

  • Tremors in the fingers
  • Scoliosis (curvature of the spine)
  • Decreased or absent deep tendon reflexes
  • Tongue fasciculations (twitching) in infants
