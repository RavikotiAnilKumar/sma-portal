What is SMA?

Spinal Muscular Atrophy (SMA) is a genetic disease affecting motor neurons and muscle strength.

It is a group of hereditary diseases that progressively destroys motor neurons—nerve cells in the brain stem and spinal cord that control essential skeletal muscle activity such as speaking, walking, breathing, and swallowing.

Why SMA Awareness Matters

Early Detection

Recognizing symptoms early can lead to timely diagnosis and treatment, which is critical for preserving motor function.

Gene Therapy

Modern treatments like gene therapy are most effective when administered before significant motor neuron loss occurs.

Community Support

Awareness reduces stigma and helps families find the support they need through multidisciplinary care teams.

Key Facts About SMA

1 in 10k

Live births affected by SMA globally.

1 in 50

People are genetic carriers of the SMA gene.

Type 1

Accounts for about 60% of all new SMA cases.

#1 Cause

SMA is the top genetic cause of infant mortality.
